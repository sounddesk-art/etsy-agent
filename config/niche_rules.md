# Etsy Agent — Niche Selection Rules

## Objective
Use eRank data and Etsy market signals to find niches with meaningful demand, manageable competition, strong long-tail potential, series/bundle potential, shop fit and low IP risk.

## Collect When Available
- Search volume
- Clicks / CTR
- Competition / listing count
- Trend direction
- Seasonality
- Related long-tail keywords
- Typical price range
- Visual quality of competition
- Bundle / series potential
- Product-format fit
- IP / trademark risk

## Opportunity Score (100)
- Demand: 30
- Competition / Supply: 25
- Shop Fit: 15
- Series / Bundle Potential: 15
- Seasonality / Timing: 10
- IP Safety: 5

## Decision
- GO NOW: 75–100
- WATCHLIST: 60–74
- REJECT: <60
Any hard IP risk overrides the score and forces REJECT.

## Long-tail Preference
Prefer clear buyer-intent phrases instead of broad terms.

## Hard Reject
Reject niches dependent on famous characters, movie/TV/game franchises, celebrity likeness, sports branding, logos, protected slogans, recognizable costumes/uniforms or copyrighted identities.

## Safe Archetypes
Original generic wizard, witch, pirate, knight, astronaut, vampire, dragon, fairy, superhero archetype, magical school or haunted castle are acceptable if not recognizable as protected IP.

## Multiplication Check
Before accepting a niche, ask whether it can produce at least 3 useful products without filler. If not, lower the score.
