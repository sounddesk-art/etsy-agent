# Etsy Agent — V1 Style Guide

## Style 1 — Illustrated
Best for humorous animals, costumed animals, seasonal themes, cards and PNGs.
Maintain consistent line quality, palette, detail level and composition inside a collection.

## Style 2 — Photorealistic
Best for animal portraits, plants, landscapes, nature prints and premium cards.
Maintain credible lighting, textures, framing and mood. Avoid plastic or uncanny AI appearance.

## Collection Rule
Approved motifs should expand into coherent series before switching aesthetics.

# Non-Negotiable Copyright / IP Rule
Never imitate or closely reproduce copyrighted characters, franchises, logos, costumes, props, typography, trade dress or other recognizable protected identities.

Generic archetypes are allowed:
- Generic wizard: YES
- Generic magical student: YES
- Wizard clearly resembling Harry Potter: NO
- Generic superhero: YES
- Character clearly resembling Batman / Spider-Man / Marvel / DC: NO
- Generic cute fantasy creature: YES
- Recognizable Pokémon-like franchise character: NO

Do not use "inspired by" as a loophole.
If an ordinary buyer would likely say "that is basically [famous character/franchise]", reject and redesign it.
When uncertain, change silhouette, clothing, props, colors, symbols, setting and composition until the concept is independently original.
