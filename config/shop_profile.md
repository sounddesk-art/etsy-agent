# Etsy Agent — Shop Profile

## Focus
Digital products around animals, humor and nature:
- Animals
- Humor
- Nature, plants and landscapes
- Greeting cards
- PNG / clipart
- Printable wall art
- Small themed bundles

## V1 Styles
- Illustrated: target ~60%
- Photorealistic: target ~40%
Future styles (disabled until explicitly enabled): Retro, Futuristic, Cozy.

## Shop Cohesion
Do not create a random collection of unrelated AI images.
Prefer coherent mini-collections of 3–10 related motifs with consistent visual language.
A buyer who likes one item should find more products in the same style.

## Publishing
V1 creates Etsy DRAFT listings only. Never publish live without explicit authorization.

## Asset Reuse
Strong motifs may be reused across suitable formats (card, PNG, printable, bundle) when this creates genuine buyer value.
Avoid filler and near-duplicates.

## Priority Order
1. Quality
2. Commercial usefulness / conversion potential
3. IP safety
4. Visual consistency
5. Token and generation efficiency
